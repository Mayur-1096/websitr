Gravity does the launching for you.

Past geostationary altitude, centrifugal force outpulls gravity. Anything anchored to the tether above that point is flung outward, not down — which is what holds the whole structure taut.

The tether

A braided carbon-nanotube ribbon, four centimeters wide, running unbroken from sea level to a mass anchor 100,000 kilometers out. It has no moving parts of its own.

The climber

Cargo pods grip the ribbon with paired rollers and pull themselves upward, drawing power from a ground-based laser rather than carrying propellant.

Four stages, one ribbon.

Ascent

Release
