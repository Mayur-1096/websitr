About Spider-Man

Spider-Man is one of Marvel's most recognizable superheroes. The character was created by writer Stan Lee and artist Steve Ditko and first appeared in Marvel Comics in 1962.

Over the years, Spider-Man has appeared in numerous animated and live-action adaptations. The movies have introduced different versions of Peter Parker as well as characters such as Miles Morales.

The Spider-Man films explore themes of responsibility, friendship, identity, sacrifice, and what it means to use extraordinary abilities for others.
